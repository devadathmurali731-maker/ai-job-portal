JOB DESCRIPTION

Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: InsightSpark Analytics
Location: Kochi, Kerala, India
Employment Type: Full-time

JOB SUMMARY

We are looking for a Data Analyst to analyze business data, create
reports and dashboards, identify trends, and provide actionable
insights to business stakeholders.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

1–3 years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence,
reporting, or a related field.

REQUIRED SKILLS

- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Data Analysis
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- Statistical Analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Power Query

PREFERRED SKILLS

- Python
- Pandas
- Tableau
- PostgreSQL
- Business Intelligence
- Data Modeling

EDUCATION

- Bachelor's degree in Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer
  Science, Business Administration, Commerce, or a related field.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Analyze business datasets.
- Clean and transform raw data.
- Write SQL queries to extract data.
- Create Power BI dashboards.
- Prepare regular business reports.
- Identify trends and patterns.
- Develop key performance indicators.
- Communicate insights to stakeholders.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

CERTIFICATIONS

Power BI, Excel, SQL, analytics, or business intelligence
certifications are preferred.
