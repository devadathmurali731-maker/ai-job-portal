JOB DESCRIPTION

Job Title: Data Scientist
Company: InsightAI Technologies
Location: Bangalore, India
Employment Type: Full-time

JOB SUMMARY

We are looking for a Data Scientist to analyze complex datasets and
develop machine learning solutions that support business decision-making.
The candidate will work with data engineering, product, and business
teams to build predictive models and analytical solutions.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

2–5 years of experience in data science, machine learning, or a related
analytical role.

REQUIRED SKILLS

- Python
- SQL
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- Machine Learning
- Statistics
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Feature Engineering

PREFERRED SKILLS

- Power BI
- Tableau
- XGBoost
- TensorFlow
- AWS
- Docker

EDUCATION

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics,
  Mathematics, Economics, Data Science, or a related field.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Analyze large datasets to identify business trends.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Develop and evaluate machine learning models.
- Perform feature engineering.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis.
- Communicate analytical findings to stakeholders.
- Create data visualizations and reports.
- Collaborate with data engineers and business teams.

CERTIFICATIONS

Data science, machine learning, cloud computing, or analytics
certifications are preferred but not mandatory.
