JOB DESCRIPTION

Job Title: HR Executive
Company: PeopleFirst Technologies
Location: Kochi, Kerala, India
Employment Type: Full-time

JOB SUMMARY

We are looking for an HR Executive to support recruitment, employee
onboarding, HR operations, employee engagement, and day-to-day human
resource activities.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

2–4 years of experience in human resources, recruitment, talent
acquisition, or a related role.

REQUIRED SKILLS

- Recruitment
- Talent Acquisition
- Employee Relations
- Employee Onboarding
- HR Operations
- Microsoft Excel
- Communication
- Interpersonal Skills
- Problem Solving

PREFERRED SKILLS

- HRMS
- Payroll
- Performance Management
- Employee Engagement
- Conflict Resolution
- HR Reporting

EDUCATION

- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration,
  Commerce, or a related field.
- MBA in Human Resources is preferred.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Manage recruitment activities.
- Screen resumes and shortlist candidates.
- Coordinate candidate interviews.
- Manage employee onboarding.
- Maintain employee records.
- Support performance management activities.
- Handle employee queries.
- Prepare HR reports.
- Support employee engagement activities.
- Coordinate with hiring managers.

CERTIFICATIONS

HR certifications are preferred but not mandatory.
