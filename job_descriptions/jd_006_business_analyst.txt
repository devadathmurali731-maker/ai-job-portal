JOB DESCRIPTION

Job Title: Business Analyst
Company: Business Solutions India
Location: Bangalore, India
Employment Type: Full-time

JOB SUMMARY

We are looking for a Business Analyst to gather business requirements,
analyze business processes, work with stakeholders, and support
data-driven business decisions.

REQUIRED EXPERIENCE

2–5 years of experience in business analysis, data analysis, consulting,
or a related field.

REQUIRED SKILLS

- Requirements Gathering
- Business Process Analysis
- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Stakeholder Management
- Communication
- Problem Solving
- Business Documentation

PREFERRED SKILLS

- Agile
- Scrum
- Jira
- Confluence
- Data Visualization
- Process Improvement
- User Stories
- Gap Analysis

EDUCATION

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business Administration, Economics,
  Commerce, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Gather requirements from business stakeholders.
- Analyze existing business processes.
- Identify process improvement opportunities.
- Prepare business requirement documents.
- Create user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Analyze business data using SQL and Excel.
- Develop business dashboards.
- Communicate requirements to technical teams.
- Participate in Agile meetings.
- Support business decision-making.

CERTIFICATIONS

Business analysis, Agile, Scrum, or analytics certifications
are preferred.
