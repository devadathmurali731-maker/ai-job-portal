# Candidate Ranking & Shortlisting

## 1. Overview

The Candidate Ranking & Shortlisting module is the Day 14 component of the AI Job Portal.

Its purpose is to transform ATS scoring results into a structured candidate ranking and recruiter-oriented workflow.

The module consumes the ATS scores generated by the Day 13 ATS Scoring Engine and performs:

- Candidate ranking
- Job-specific candidate ordering
- Configurable score-based classification
- Shortlisting
- Manual review identification
- Rejection classification
- Recruiter-friendly output generation

The module does not replace recruiter decision-making. It provides a structured workflow layer over the ATS scores.
