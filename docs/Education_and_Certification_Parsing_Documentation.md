# Education & Certification Parsing Documentation

## 1. Overview

The Education & Certification Parsing module is responsible for extracting, structuring, normalizing, and evaluating educational qualifications and professional certifications from resumes.

This module is part of the AI Job Portal's resume intelligence pipeline.

The module processes structured resume sections generated by the Resume Section Segmentation Engine.

---

## 2. Objective

The main objectives are:

- Extract academic qualifications from resumes.
- Identify degree types.
- Extract fields of study.
- Identify educational institutions.
- Extract graduation years.
- Extract professional certifications.
- Normalize certification names.
- Identify certification providers.
- Categorize certifications by relevance.
- Calculate education relevance for a target job role.
- Generate structured candidate education profiles.
